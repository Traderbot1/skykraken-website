Frequently asked

Evidence, answered.

What is in a SkyKraken evidence dossier?

Each dossier contains the aircraft identity, the full flight trajectory drawn on a map, the pilot position and take-off point, altitude and speed, a threat assessment, a timestamped chronology and an integrity fingerprint that proves the document has not been altered.

Is SkyKraken evidence admissible in court?

The dossier is designed to be handed to an authority such as police or courts. It is timestamped in the local time zone of the capture site and carries a tamper-evident integrity fingerprint, so its authenticity can be verified.

How does SkyKraken prove the evidence was not tampered with?

Every dossier includes a cryptographic integrity fingerprint. If a single value in the document changes, the fingerprint no longer matches — which reveals tampering and confirms authenticity.
