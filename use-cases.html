Use cases

One system, three frontlines.

SkyKraken deploys in minutes wherever drones are a threat — for those who respond, those who guard a perimeter, and those who protect a gathering.

Law enforcement

Move toward the operator.

SkyKraken locates the remote pilot so officers can intervene on the person, not chase the aircraft. It tells a hobby drone from a real threat in seconds, and documents the incursion for proceedings.

The result is admissible proof — a forensic dossier ready for a prosecutor — not just an alert that fades.

Sensitive sites

Protect the perimeter.

Prisons face contraband drops. Airport zones face incursions. Energy and industrial sites face espionage and sabotage. SkyKraken guards them all — flagging any drone entering a protection zone, instantly and geolocated.

It deploys fast, without altering existing infrastructure, and runs fully offline.

Private security & events

Secure the gathering.

Stadiums, concerts, VIP protection, private estates — the portable format follows the event. Whitelisting clears official filming drones so they never raise an alert, while every unidentified aircraft stays under watch.

Set it down, switch it on, read the airspace at a glance.
