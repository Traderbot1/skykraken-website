A typical chain on a site equipped with a radar, PTZ cameras, a C2 or VMS, and loudspeakers.

  1. Radar

    Detects and tracks a target.

  2. Horizon

    Identifies it: DJI Mavic 3, serial number, pilot at 420 m.

  3. PTZ camera

    Points at the drone, then at the pilot, to remove doubt.

  4. C2 / VMS

    Alarm “high level” with the identity and the map.

  5. Loudspeakers

    Alert message broadcast in the zone concerned.

Integration, system by system.

We adapt the station’s outputs to the format each of your systems expects. Every connector is developed by SkyKraken, delivered tested on your platform and reusable on all your sites.

ConnectorWhat it does
Alerts to your C2 or tactical pictureEach detection creates a track, an event or an alarm in your tool: position, identity, threat level.
Video management (VMS)Alarms in Milestone, Genetec or your VMS, with the drone’s identity.
Automatic PTZ pointingCameras turn on their own towards the drone, then towards the pilot (ONVIF protocol).
Radar + radio identity fusionEach radar track receives the drone’s identity and its pilot’s position when they are available.
Alert broadcastA message is played automatically over your loudspeakers according to the zone and the threat level.

An open API, documented.

Horizon sends your systems, in real time, the identity of each drone, the position of its pilot and the threat level, and can enrich your own radar tracks.

  • REST API

    Current tracks, history, zones, whitelist and evidence dossiers, in JSON.

  • Real-time stream

    WebSocket: new track, update, zone entry, threat level change, end of track.

  • Your tools’ formats

    Webhooks, MQTT, VMS events or your C2’s format: we adapt the output.

  • Clear conventions

    WGS 84 positions, UTC timestamps, stable track identifiers, authenticated access.

Sensor fusion, both ways.

The case and the SK-1 work autonomously. Horizon connects: it receives tracks from your sensors, or sends its own to your fusion engine.

  • In both directions

    Horizon takes in your radar tracks, cameras and other detectors, or feeds your own fusion engine.

  • Association by position and time

    A radar track and a radio detection of the same drone are merged into one identified track.

  • Deduplication

    A drone seen by several sources (DJI, Remote ID, several stations) appears only once.

  • Explained threat

    A score from 0 to 100 with its reasons, sent with every track.
