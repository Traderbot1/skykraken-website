What the SK-1 does.

Built to be read in a second, in daylight, with gloves on.

  • Four FPV bands monitored

    1.2 · 3.3 · 5.8 and 6.5 GHz, listened to continuously on four channels.

  • The drone’s video on screen

    7-inch screen under a sun hood: the FPV drone’s analog image, for immediate confirmation.

  • Immediate alert

    Spectrum display, confidence index, audible and visual alert.

  • Sized for the field

    191 × 218 × 55 mm, about 1 kg, shock-proof corners, built-in battery charged over USB-C, 100 % passive.
