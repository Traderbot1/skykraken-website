The cameras you own, turned into drone sensors.

Thousands of sites already run cameras on Genetec or Milestone. Eyes adds drone detection to them, in software: nothing new on your masts, nothing to finance on our side. The operator receives the alarm in the VMS they already use, with a timestamped clip.

Paired with a SkyKraken radio sensor, false alarms fall: the radio detects from afar, the camera confirms and films.

Three sources, one core, every VMS.

Video always comes in as RTSP, wherever it comes from. Detection never changes, and each output to a VMS is a small independent adapter: adding a new VMS touches neither the core nor the other integrations.

What Eyes and Trace do.

Eyes detects the drone and alerts the operator in their usual VMS. Trace turns each detection into a case file. A SkyKraken case or station becomes one more sensor that points the cameras.

Detection

Detects, classifies and tracks drones on existing video streams, and raises an alarm in Genetec or Milestone with a timestamped clip.

Management

Honest about range.

A camera sees a drone at short to medium range: below roughly ten pixels on the image, no AI can follow. Eyes is designed for compact sites such as prisons and industrial sites, and to confirm the alerts of a SkyKraken radio sensor. It does not replace a radar.

That is why the pilot measures range per camera model, on your own cameras, before anything else.

  • On site, on your servers

    Video does not leave your network. Updates and licences can be delivered by file for sites cut off from the internet.

  • Aircraft, not people

    Eyes looks for drones in the sky; it does not identify individuals.

  • A French product

    Designed in Mérignac, for buyers who need a sovereign option.
