Passive. Silent. It sees what radar can’t.

SkyKraken listens to the spectrum instead of transmitting into it. Passive detection needs no licence, causes no interference, warns no one, and still sees a drone hidden behind a building.

Radio expertise is our core. Every product in the range, from the case to camera software, is built on it, and our engineers adapt the outputs to the systems you already run.

This is what our expertise looks like.

Every drone talks to its pilot over radio. Our engineers read each signature in the spectrum: DJI OcuSync hopping across 2.4 GHz, Remote ID beacons, the continuous carrier of an analog FPV drone at 5.8 GHz. That is how SkyKraken names a drone without ever transmitting.

Drone detection, answered.

How does SkyKraken detect drones?

With passive radio-frequency detection. SkyKraken listens to the signals drones and their remote controls exchange, emits nothing, and works without line of sight.

Can SkyKraken locate the pilot?

Yes. For DJI drones, SkyKraken shows the positions of the drone, the pilot and the take-off point, with the model and serial number. Drones broadcasting European Remote ID are identified as well.

Which product should I choose?

Mobile for events and temporary sites, Horizon for permanent sites that need integration, SK-1 for agents facing FPV drones, Eyes to add drone detection to the cameras you already have. Many sites combine them.

Does it integrate with our C2, VMS and radar?

Horizon exposes a REST and WebSocket API, webhooks and MQTT. We develop the connectors for your C2, Milestone or Genetec, ONVIF PTZ cameras, radar fusion and alert broadcast, and deliver them tested on your platform.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything runs locally. The Mobile case works with no internet and no connection, with an offline map of all of France, and your data stays in the case.

What evidence do we get?

For every flight, a PDF dossier with the classification reasons, trajectory, timestamped chronology and a SHA-256 fingerprint for the chain of custody, exported on USB.
