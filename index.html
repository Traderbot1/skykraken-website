Why RF detection

Passive. Silent. It sees what radar can't.

SkyKraken listens to the radio spectrum instead of emitting into it. Passive RF detection needs no licence, causes no interference, and works without line of sight — so a drone hidden behind a building is still seen.

It is also the only detection method that can locate the pilot. Larger brands such as DJI and Autel use stronger transmitters, which means they are detected earlier and farther.

Frequently asked

Drone detection, answered.

What is SkyKraken?

SkyKraken is a portable drone detection, identification and tracking system that spots drones around a site, locates the pilot, and generates an evidence dossier — with no infrastructure and no Internet connection.

How does SkyKraken detect drones?

SkyKraken uses fully passive radio-frequency detection. It listens to the radio spectrum for the signals drones broadcast and exchange, so it emits nothing, needs no licence, and detects drones even without line of sight — unlike radar or cameras.

Can SkyKraken locate a drone's pilot?

Yes. For compatible drones, SkyKraken shows the remote pilot's position and the take-off point in addition to the drone's position, so ground teams can move toward the operator, not just the aircraft.

Which drones does SkyKraken detect?

SkyKraken identifies DJI and Autel drones among the most widespread brands, plus any drone compliant with the Remote ID standard, and reports the make, model and unique device identifier.

Does SkyKraken work without Internet?

Yes. The system is fully self-contained: it runs offline, starts automatically and deploys in minutes, which makes it suitable for nomadic field use, patrols and temporary posts.

Does SkyKraken provide court-usable evidence?

SkyKraken generates a timestamped forensic dossier with a mapped trajectory, pilot location, threat assessment and an integrity fingerprint, designed to be handed to an authority such as police or courts.
