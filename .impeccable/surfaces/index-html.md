Scope: home page (index.html, fr/index.html) and new product pages (Mobile, Horizon, SK-1, Eyes) in EN and FR; existing inner pages inherit the new system. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a night topographic survey sheet on which radio waves are drawn: SkyKraken's expertise is shown as RF propagating across real terrain, not as a radar HUD or a SaaS hero. Refuses the category default of neon radar sweeps over a dark dashboard and the split hero with product shot.
OWN-WORLD: Navy night ground (#0A0E1A family), cream contour linework and cream "paper" sheets, insignia red reserved for threats, pilots and the primary action. Cartographic lettering in Archivo (expanded for display, condensed for map labels), JetBrains Mono only for coordinates, frequencies and measurements. Thin 1px rules, map legend boxes, grid ticks, WGS 84 coordinates.
STORY: The visitor sees a drone appear on the terrain, get identified and linked to its pilot; understands the four products as four ways of covering the same map (carried, fixed, in hand, through cameras); believes the evidence is real; requests a demonstration.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed live Three.js contour relief seen obliquely; a Horizon mast emits expanding RF wavefronts across the relief; a drone crosses, a cartographic label resolves (model, serial), a dashed red line drops to the pilot on the ground.
