Everything an operator needs, nothing they have to configure.

From the first alert to the dossier handed to investigators, every step is on the same touch screen.

  • Identification

    DJI drones: model, serial number, positions of the drone, the pilot and the take-off point. European Remote ID over WiFi and Bluetooth.

  • Zones to protect

    The operator draws zones on the map, as a circle or a polygon. Any drone entering one raises an alert.

  • Friendly drones on a whitelist

    Authorised drones, from the police, contractors or the event’s videographer, stay visible but trigger no alert.

  • Voice and audible alerts

    Every new drone and every intrusion is announced aloud, so the operator knows even without looking at the screen.

  • Threat classifier

    A score from 0 to 100 in four levels, low to critical, explained rule by rule: zone, altitude, speed, approach, night flight.

  • Legal evidence

    For each flight, a PDF with the classification reasons, trajectory, timestamped chronology and a SHA-256 fingerprint, exported on USB.

  • Calendar of captured flights

    Each flight is filed under its day, live. Find a flight by date and export one flight or a whole period.

  • Made for the field

    Automatic start, touch screen, offline map of all of France, interface usable by a non-technician.

The operator’s screen.

Range rings, threat scoring, pilot location and the evidence dossier, built to be read at a glance. This interactive demo runs on simulated data.

6 hoursTo report an overflight

Since 1 November 2026, the interministerial instruction against malicious drones asks sites to report every unauthorised overflight to the Interior Ministry’s national centralisation point within six hours. The case keeps what the report needs: time, positions, model, serial number, trajectory and threat level.
