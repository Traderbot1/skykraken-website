Detection

Passive RF, multiple protocols at once.

Multi-protocol detection

SkyKraken listens across multiple identification protocols simultaneously — broadcast Remote ID and the proprietary signals of major manufacturers — so a drone is seen whether it announces itself or not.

Fully passive

It emits nothing. No licence, no interference, no warning to the drone — and it works without line of sight, where radar and cameras are blind in urban terrain.

DJI, Autel & Remote ID

Recognises DJI and Autel — among the most widespread brands — plus any Remote ID-compliant aircraft, reporting make, model and the unique device identifier.

Identify & track

Real-time identification & telemetry

For every detected drone, SkyKraken shows position, altitude, speed, heading and trajectory in real time, updated continuously while the aircraft transmits — the moment it is captured, with no artificial latency.

Pilot & take-off location

RF is the only modality that reveals the operator. Beyond the drone, SkyKraken maps the remote pilot's position and the take-off point — so ground teams move toward the person, not just the aircraft.

Decide

Know where to look, instantly.

Live offline tactical map

Drones, pilots, take-off points and protection zones, live, on a basemap that works with no connection — designed to be read at a glance by police or security officers.

Automatic threat scoring

A classification engine scores every drone from low to critical, using altitude, proximity, flight behaviour and time of day — so the operator focuses on the real threat instead of triaging alerts.

Whitelist & protection zones

Clear legitimate operators so they stay visible without raising alerts, and define protection zones whose breach is flagged instantly and geolocated.

Prove

Every flight, a case file.

On demand, SkyKraken generates a complete forensic dossier — aircraft identity, full mapped trajectory, pilot position, threat assessment, timestamped chronology and an integrity fingerprint, in the local time zone of the capture site.
